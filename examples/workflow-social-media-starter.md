Voice is warm, clear, and practical; avoid pressure and health promises. Use navy and cream. The fictional brief permits the statement “Başlangıç seviyesi dersimizle tanışın”; it does not supply price, capacity, instructor credentials, booking URL, or testimonials. Final images and their rights evidence are missing. Baseline and analytics access are unavailable. User authorization permits drafting only.

## 1. Social Media Strategist: strategy-v1

Use an introduction carousel, a short instructor-introduction video concept, and a question sticker Story concept. Prioritize the carousel first. Primary KPI is qualified profile visits during the campaign window, using the account's available metric definition; saves per reached account is a secondary diagnostic metric. Request the previous comparable week's export before setting numeric growth targets. Do not infer business conversions from profile visits.

Open questions for the owner: confirm the real account, schedule, class details, CTA destination, final assets and rights. Missing destination/offer details block booking copy; the introductory draft can proceed.
